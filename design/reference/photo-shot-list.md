# Photos needed

Six groups, six photos each — **36 files**. Drop them in the folder named below.
More is better (up to 10 per group); fewer works too, minimum 2 per group.

Every photo is used more than once: the first becomes the community's cover, the
rest become its gallery, and the events inside that community reuse the same
pool. That is why 36 files can dress 123 slots.

## Specs (all six groups)

- Landscape, roughly **3:2**. At least **1600px** wide; 2400px is ideal.
- **JPG**. Under 1 MB each — send bigger and I will compress them.
- **No text, watermarks or logos** anywhere in the frame.
- **Darker, quieter frames work best.** The site is near-black. Bright midday
  holiday shots fight it and look like stock.

## The six groups

### 1. `sailing-monaco` — Cap Ferrat Sailing Society, Monaco
Older wooden sailing boats. A marina before sunrise, deck and rigging detail,
rope, crew working on deck, the sea from on board, the harbour at dusk.

### 2. `sailing-ibiza` — Es Freus Passage, Ibiza
A boat under sail in clear water, anchored in a quiet cove, the sail from below,
coastline from the water. Warmer and lighter than Monaco.

### 3. `cold-aspen` — The Cold Room, Aspen
Ice bath, a frozen lake at first light, sauna interior, steam, a wooden deck in
snow, someone in cold water. Cold blues and dark wood.

### 4. `dining-lisbon` — The Bica Table, Lisbon
One long table laid for dinner, cooking over open fire, candlelight, wine
poured, a plated dish, a small dining room. Warm and dark.

### 5. `padel-dubai` — The Sunrise Court, Dubai
A padel or tennis court at dawn, floodlights, rackets and balls, players mid
match, a breakfast table afterwards.

### 6. `art-bangkok` — Nightform, Bangkok
An artist's studio at night, a warehouse gallery, work in progress, materials
and paint, a lit interior seen from outside.

## Where to get them

Unsplash or Pexels are both free for commercial use and need no attribution.
Search the words above. Your own photos are better if you have them.

Tell me. I will resize and compress them, write captions that match what is
actually in each picture, wire them to the right communities and events, check
how they look at phone and desktop size, and deploy.
